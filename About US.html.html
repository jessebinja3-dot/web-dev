About Us

Welcome to Neverland high school, a modern school located in the heart of Tokyo, Japan.

Our school provides a friendly and international learning environment where students can develop their knowledge, skills, and confidence.

Our History

Neverland High School was established to provide high-quality education to students from Japan and around the world.

Over the years, our school has created a diverse community where students from different cultures can learn together.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide quality education and prepare students for their future careers and academic goals.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a leading international school in Tokyo that encourages innovation, creativity, respect, and lifelong learning.

Why Choose Us?

  • Qualified and experienced teachers
  • Modern learning environment
  • International student community
  • Practical learning opportunities
  • Supportive and friendly atmosphere
